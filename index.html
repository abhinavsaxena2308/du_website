About Bennett University

Bennett University, established by The Times Group, is among the best private universities in India, offering globally benchmarked, research-driven, and industry-aligned education. The university offers specialised programs spanning Engineering, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Management, Media, Law, Liberal Arts, Design, and Applied Sciences.
